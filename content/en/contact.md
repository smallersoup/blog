This blog is maintained by 小碗汤. For corrections, broken links, copyright concerns or privacy issues, use the [blog repository's GitHub Issues](https://github.com/smallersoup/blog/issues), the author's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/smallersoup), or the site's existing [WeChat QR code](/img/wechat-qr-code.jpg).

For technical reports, include the article URL, software version, reproduction steps and redacted error output.

GitHub Issues and article comments are public. Do not post passwords, access tokens, identity documents or other people's personal information. Use a private communication channel for sensitive reports.

For an attribution or copyright concern, identify the page, the material in question and the source of the rights claim so that it can be reviewed.
