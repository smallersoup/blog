Updated on October 31, 2026. This policy applies to kubeinfo.cn, maintained by 小碗汤. Use the [contact page](/en/contact/) for privacy questions.

## Website delivery and local storage

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Cloudflare network services. Requests for pages and resources expose information needed to deliver them, such as IP address, request time, URL and browser information. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

The site uses browser local storage for interface preferences such as light or dark appearance. You can clear this data in your browser settings.

## Google advertising

The site includes Google AdSense code. Actual advertising depends on account approval, user choices, region and advertising availability.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this site or other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet. Advertising services may also use web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to deliver and measure ads and prevent invalid traffic.

Manage personalized advertising through [Google ad settings](https://myadcenter.google.com/). You can also visit [AboutAds choices](https://optout.aboutads.info/) to opt out of personalized advertising cookies from participating third-party vendors. Opting out of personalization does not remove all advertising. See [how Google uses information from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Analytics

The site configures Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Baidu Analytics, Umami and a page-view counter. These services may process visited URLs, referral information, browser and device information, timestamps, cookies or other identifiers. Clarity may also process page interactions and session replay data. Google Analytics is loaded through ga.kubeinfo.cn; using a site subdomain does not change the underlying Google service.

Collection and retention depend on each service and its configuration. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement), [Baidu's privacy policy](https://privacy.baidu.com/policy) and [Umami's privacy policy](https://umami.is/privacy).

The privacy, about and contact pages do not load the site's configured advertising or analytics tracking scripts.

## Comments, notifications and external resources

Comments use [Giscus](https://giscus.app/) and GitHub Discussions. Comments are public, and GitHub's policies apply to sign-in and comment data. Do not post sensitive information.

The site configures Webpushr for browser notifications. Manage or revoke notification permission in your browser. See [Webpushr's privacy policy](https://www.webpushr.com/privacy-policy).

Images, fonts and some scripts may be served by external providers such as jsDelivr. Loading them sends requests to those providers. Linked websites have their own privacy policies.

## Your choices and questions

You can manage cookies, local storage and notification permissions in your browser, and use the advertising choices linked above. Some settings may affect comments or interface preferences. Choices may need to be set separately on different devices and browsers.

To raise a site-related data concern, provide the relevant page and a description through the [contact page](/en/contact/). Avoid putting sensitive information in a public report.
